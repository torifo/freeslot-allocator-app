FRELOCATOR is a planning app for organizing tasks, daily schedules, and weekly reviews.

What to include in a support request

  • Platform: Web, Android, or macOS
  • App version
  • What you expected to happen
  • What actually happened
  • Steps to reproduce the issue

Current scope

The current app stores planning data locally on the device. If data appears to be missing, first check whether the app data was cleared or the app was reinstalled on that device.
